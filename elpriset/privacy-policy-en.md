# Privacy Policy for Elpriset

_Last updated: 12 September 2026_

## Summary
Elpriset does not collect, store or share any personal data.

## What data is processed?
The app requires no account and no sign-in. We do not collect your name,
email, location or any other personal data.

## Settings
Your choices in the app (price area and fees such as grid transfer, energy
tax, markup and VAT) are stored only locally on your device and never leave
your phone.

## Network requests
To display electricity prices, the app fetches price data from the service
Elpriset just nu (https://www.elprisetjustnu.se). Each request contains only
the selected date and price area — nothing that can identify you.

## Tracking and ads
The app contains no tracking, no ads and no third-party analytics SDKs.

## Contact
Questions about this policy: support@kldev.se
