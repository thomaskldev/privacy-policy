## Description (max 4000)

```
RadioRun plays the radio stations you actually listen to, right from your iPhone.

ADD YOUR OWN CHANNELS
There is no fixed station list to scroll through. You add the streams you want by pasting
in a stream address, giving the channel a name, a short label and a colour. Test the stream
right in the form before you save it.

TAKE IT ON YOUR RUN
The Apple Watch app is a full player, not a remote control. Start and stop playback, switch
channels, and pause with a Double Tap without touching the screen. With Bluetooth
headphones and a cellular Apple Watch or Wi-Fi, you can leave your phone at home. Put the Quick Play widget in your Smart Stack or on your watch face and a
single tap starts your channel.

KEEPS PLAYING
Audio continues in the background and on the Lock Screen, with proper artwork and controls
in Control Center and on your headphones.

YOUR PHONE AND WATCH STAY IN STEP
Channels and your selection sync directly between your iPhone and Apple Watch. No account,
no sign-in, no server in the middle.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts. No analytics. No tracking. No ads. Everything you create stays on your devices.

AVAILABLE IN 28 LANGUAGES
Including all 24 official EU languages and regional English variants. The app follows your
device language automatically.
```
