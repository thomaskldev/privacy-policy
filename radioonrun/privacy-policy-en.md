# Privacy Policy — Radio On Run

**Last updated: 23 September 2026**

Radio On Run is developed by Klasson Development. This policy explains what the app does
with your information. The short version: the app has no servers, no accounts and no
analytics, and it does not collect anything about you.

## Data we collect

**None.** Radio On Run does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. There is no
user account, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics or tracking framework of any kind.

## Data stored on your device

The app stores the following locally on your iPhone and Apple Watch:

- The radio channels you create: name, short name, colour and stream address.
- Which channels you have chosen to show, and the order you put them in.

This is kept in the app's own storage area on the device (a shared app group so the watch
app and its widget can read it). It is included in your device backup if you back up your
device, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

## Synchronisation between your devices

If you use the Apple Watch app, your channels and selections are sent between your own
iPhone and your own Apple Watch using Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. This is a direct
link between your two paired devices. The data does not pass through any server operated by
us, and we cannot see it.

## Radio streams and third parties

When you play a channel, your device connects directly to the stream address you entered.
That connection is between your device and whoever operates that stream — we are not
involved and receive nothing.

Please note that the stream operator will see your device's IP address and network
information, exactly as with any website you visit. Their handling of that information is
governed by their own privacy policy, not this one. You choose which addresses to add, so
you choose which operators you connect to.

## Tracking

Radio On Run does not track you. It does not use the App Tracking Transparency framework
because there is nothing to ask permission for, and it shares no data with data brokers or
advertising networks.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
Note that the app plays whatever stream address you enter; we do not control or review the
content of third-party streams.

## Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct or erase.
To remove everything the app has stored, delete the app from your device.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address and the
date at the top will be revised.
