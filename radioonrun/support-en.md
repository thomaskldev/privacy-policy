# Radio On Run — Support

Radio On Run plays the radio streams you add yourself, on iPhone and Apple Watch — built
for listening while you run.

**Contact:** radioonrun@kldev.se
We usually reply within a few days. Please include your iPhone and Apple Watch models and
the iOS/watchOS versions if you are reporting a problem.

**Requires:** iOS 26.5 or later. The watch app requires watchOS 26.5 or later.

---

## Frequently asked questions

### Where do I find a stream address?

Many radio stations publish their stream address on their website, often under "Listen",
"Stream" or "Other players". It is a web address that typically ends in `.mp3`, `.aac`,
`.pls` or `.m3u8`. Paste it into **Add Channel…** and tap **Test Channel** to check it plays
before saving.

### The app is empty when I open it

That is intentional — Radio On Run has no built-in station list. Tap the slider icon in the
top right, then **Add Channel…** to add your first channel.

### My channels don't show up on the watch

Channels are created on the iPhone and sent to the watch. Open the iPhone app once with the
watch nearby and both devices paired; the list then appears on the watch. You do not need
the iPhone with you afterwards.

### The watch says "Connect Bluetooth headphones to play"

Apple Watch plays audio only through Bluetooth headphones or speakers, not through its own
speaker. Connect your headphones in Settings → Bluetooth on the watch and try again.

### How do I put Radio On Run in the Smart Stack?

On the watch face, turn the Digital Crown to open the Smart Stack, scroll down and tap
**Edit**, then tap **+** and choose **Radio On Run → Quick Play**. Tapping the widget opens
the app and starts your first channel. You can also add it as a complication on the watch
face.

### Can I pause without touching the watch?

Yes. On the Now Playing screen, use **Double Tap** (tap your index finger and thumb together
twice) to play or pause.

### How do I reorder or remove channels?

In **Select Channels** on the iPhone, tap **Edit** to drag channels into order. Swipe left
on a channel to delete it, or swipe right to edit it.

### A channel stopped working

Stream addresses are run by the station, not by us, and they sometimes change. Check the
station's website for a current address and update the channel by swiping right on it in
**Select Channels**.

### Does the app collect any data?

No. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. Everything stays on your own
devices. See the privacy policy for details.
